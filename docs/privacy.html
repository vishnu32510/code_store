Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how codestore ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the codestore mobile application.

1) Information We Collect

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Depending on how you use our app, we may collect the following data:

Account information

  • Email address and basic account identifiers when you sign in with email, Google, or Apple.
  • Basic profile information provided by your sign-in provider (such as display name and profile photo URL).

Application and usage data

  • Preferences and user state stored locally on your device to ensure a persistent user experience.
  • Basic technical logs to help resolve crashes and monitor app reliability.

2) How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and securely sign you in.
  • To maintain your preferred application settings (such as dark theme preferences).
  • To resolve technical failures and ensure overall app stability.

3) Third-Party Services

The application integrates the following third-party platform tools:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore: Synchronized database storage for user metadata.

4) Device Permissions

  • Photo Library / Storage access: Used only when you explicitly select photos to upload or choose to save files to the device.

5) Data Deletion

We respect your right to control your data. You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time from within the profile settings screen of the app. This action immediately deletes your user records from Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

6) Security

We use industry-standard secure socket layer (SSL/TLS) encryption to protect your data in transit and adhere to modern security practices to keep your data safe.

7) Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@codestoreapp.com.
