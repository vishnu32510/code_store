Code Store App Help Center

Need assistance with Code Store App? We are here to help you resolve issues related to account authentication, database synchronization, app crashes, or other bug reports.

Contact Support

Our typical response time is 1 to 2 business days.

Information to Include in Your Request

  • Device Model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro, Samsung S24).
  • Operating System Version (e.g., iOS 17.4, Android 14).
  • App Version Number (e.g., 1.0.0).
  • A clear description of the issue, including steps to reproduce it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my database not syncing?

Please check your internet connection and ensure you are fully authenticated. Firebase updates require connection to synchronize data.

How do I change my theme?

Go to your user profile screen and toggle the theme switcher to easily swap between dark and light modes.

How can I delete my data?

You can delete your account entirely by choosing "Delete Account" in the profile settings, which immediately purges all user data.
