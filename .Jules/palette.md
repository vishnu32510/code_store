## 2026-09-14 - Add Tooltips to Icon-Only Action Buttons\n**Learning:** Icon-only floating action buttons lacking semantic labels or tooltips present an accessibility challenge for screen reader users and web/desktop users who rely on hover states. Adding a simple `tooltip` property to a `FloatingActionButton` resolves this issue elegantly without visual clutter.\n**Action:** Always ensure icon-only buttons (`IconButton`, `FloatingActionButton`) have a descriptive `tooltip` or `semanticsLabel` attribute to enhance accessibility and usability across all target platforms.
